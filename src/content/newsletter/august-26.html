Brief independent investigation of agents’ behavior, reasoning and collaboration in the OpenAI / Hugging Face hacking incident
METR, Redwood Research, August 2026

  • A 3rd party audit of the Hugging Face hack. This follows a separate presentation given by OpenAI engineers. For a plain-language summary, I'd suggest comparing Dwarkesh's and Cal Newport's overviews. I think Dwarkesh's language is overly dramatic while Cal is too dismissive of loss-of-control concerns.
  • Last month I shared an article about the hack. In hindsight, its description of the event is mostly wrong. That article framed the attack as a one-off accident conducted by a single model. In reality, the hack was one piece of a multi-day effort coordinated by hundreds of OpenAI models (without OpenAI's awareness). Notably, the agents found ways to alter their tool calls in an attempt to hide their cheating.
